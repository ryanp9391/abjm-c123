## Precision control and what ran where

* **Rank/membership thresholds.** Double-precision spans are unreliable here: the word bras have column norms 1e-8…1e16 and the normalised float matrix gives 411/440 instead of the exact 400 when the cut is chosen naively (noise columns). Therefore *every* statement about ranks, localised dimensions and (non-)membership at 4 and 6 sites is the exact modular one (p = 4194301, repeated with p = 4189277 for the L = 3 layers ℓ ≤ 4 and at three θ-points; accidental rank drop probability ≲ rank/p ≈ 1e-4 per rank, and two primes agree). Float (numpy) was used only for exploration, sparse searches and the individually-localised-words scan (defect < 1e-9 of the bra norm).
* **mpmath, 30 digits, 4 sites** (exact `Fraction` bras of the 11 independent ≤ 1-factor words and 41 independent ≤ 2-factor words, modified Gram–Schmidt at dps = 30): relative residual of T1 = 0.63, 0.12, 0.090, 0.31 and T2 = 0.049, 0.017, 0.13 for ≤ 1 factor (failure is not rounding), 7e-23, 1.8e-23, 3e-24, 8e-24 (T1) and 3.4e-24, 2.8e-24, 1.9e-23 (T2) for ≤ 2 factors (success; limited by the 41-word conditioning); T3a: 5e-30 at ℓ = 1.
* **6 sites**: exact rational arithmetic (python `Fraction`, one pass 15–25 s) for the identities of the controls section (calibration with c₁, c₁₂, c₃) at three θ-points, and for the explicit coefficient expansions of T1/T2 in the 5-vector basis; exact modular for all spans. (The mpmath 30-digit single-word check at 6 sites was replaced by the stronger exact-rational one.)
* **What failed / not done**: Wolfram kernel unavailable (licence expired; all `lc` cells unrun); MIP solver crashed (memory); L = 4 pairs (65536-dim) not attempted (the claim ℓ_min = 2(L−1) rests on L = 2, 3); the zero-test of factors is exact (zeros of the prefactor g).
