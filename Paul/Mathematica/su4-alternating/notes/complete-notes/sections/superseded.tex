\section{Superseded or corrected statements}\label{sec:superseded}
%======================================================================

\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{Singular point.} Bridge tables, degeneracy analyses and the twisted-shift fix $\hat S$ at $\theta_A=\theta_F$ are artefacts
  of the singular point. They are replaced by the ABJM point (\S\ref{sec:abjm}).
\item ``The SoV basis survives at the ABJM point with shifted evaluation points'': the shifted words are a basis but not
  $B$-eigen. ``The Ryan--Volin $B$ is degenerate at the ABJM point'' is resolved by the regularised ($d\to2h$) basis
  (\S\ref{sec:sov}).
\item Per-length product-state searches for P, LP and LR are superseded: the three-point bras must be length independent
  (\S\ref{sec:configs}).
\item \emph{Odd-bridge formula:} the factor $1/(1-z_4/z_1)$ was missing in an early version (corrected 11-10-2026).
\item \emph{Even bridges:} ``the even bridge is not reached by localising words; the F-site gap'' (notes of 12 Oct, \S6) is
  solved by the pair $\kappa$-family at the ABJM point with $z_2=z_3$ (\S\ref{sec:kappa}). The generic-$\theta$ locality
  searches failed because $t_2(\theta_F+h)$ localises only at the ABJM point.
\item \emph{Paul's formula:} ``ask Paul for his twisted-vacuum overlap formula'' (plan of 13 Oct, \S0) was based on an unclear
  object. Then, on the morning of 13 Oct, ``$\braket{W_a^L}\Psi$ has no Q-form'' was concluded from a bra-level argument. That
  conclusion is withdrawn: on the relevant sectors the overlap equals a twisted-vacuum overlap (Proposition~1,
  \S\ref{sec:qform}). The bra-level statement (no twist sharing the SoV basis has $\bra{W_a^L}$ as eigenbra) remains true.
\item \emph{Reference states:} ``$K_\kappa$ fails on $\bra0_{\rm comp}$'' is refined. The five-word coefficients are specific to the
  $W$ class; block-BPS references admit class-dependent words but have vanishing cross pairings (\S\ref{sec:admiss}).
  ``Changing the reference to $\bra{0_w}$'' (route A of 13 Oct) fails, since no $\kappa$-family exists on it at $z_2\ne z_3$,
  and it is not needed.
\item \emph{Pair factor:} ``$K_{\kappa=1}(\tau)=0$ for all non-vacuum states'' is false; $K_1$ vanishes only for $n\le1$. The
  claimed even/odd ratio relation does not exist (\S\ref{sec:qform-limit}).
\item \emph{Untwisting sign:} the untwisting of a rotated pair uses $D=\mathrm{diag}(c-\varphi_j)$, not $c+\varphi_j$
  (\S\ref{sec:admiss}).
\item \emph{Rerun of 13 Oct:} in \nb{pair-group-rotation.wb} (cell 17), four two-factor pair words localise with constants
  $(51/31)$ or $(71/31)\times\prod\lambda_k$, not $\prod\lambda_k$; the python only fitted the constant. $K_\kappa$ is unaffected.
  In \nb{even-bridge-lpairs.wb} \S2 the F1 and F2 labels of the untwisting table are swapped relative to the text; the limits
  are the same.
\item \emph{Norm:} the derivation of the SoV norm $\bbB$ in Q-functions (agent E, 13 Oct) was unnecessary, since $\bbB$ is known
  (P.~Ryan). It is kept as a cross-check (\S\ref{sec:qform-norm}).
\end{itemize}
