\begin{abstract}
\noindent These notes collect everything obtained in the \nb{su4-alternating} thread of the \nb{abjm-c123} repository
between 11 and 13 October 2026. They supersede the 9-page notes of 12 October
(\nb{notes/abjm-transfer-matrices-symmetries.tex}). The subject is the $\su(4)$ alternating chain
$\mathbf 4\otimes\bar{\mathbf 4}\otimes\cdots$ at the ABJM point $\theta_A-\theta_F=2h$, and the tree-level structure
constants of the ABJM note in the full $\su(4)$ sector. The background parts treat one-pass fused transfer matrices,
the identification of the ABJM point, localising transfer-matrix words, twisted vacua, highest-weight states and
Q-functions, discrete symmetries and selection rules, two-block polarisation forms, and the companion-twist
separation-of-variables basis with its dictionary, dual basis and regularisation at the ABJM point. The structure-constant
parts give closed odd-bridge formulas at any length, a common pair rotation (the pair $\kappa$-family) that produces the
even bridges with $M\in\{1,L-1,L\}$, and a Q-function form of the $M=1$ (equivalently $M=L-1$) even-bridge structure
constant. The middle even bridges $2\le M\le L-2$, first present at $L=4$, remain open. The Q-form rests on two facts: the overlaps of
the reference bra $\bra{W_a^L}$ coincide with those of a companion twisted vacuum, and they factorise over the two middle
twist eigenvalues into a polynomial in the F-site single-Q ratios. The resulting formula reproduces the data at
$L=2,3$, with an analytic untwisted limit at $L=2$.
\end{abstract}
